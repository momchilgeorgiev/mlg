# About

I've heard a lot of concerns from a few people that I know about how bad LLMs are for the environment, mainly the use of *water*. I researched it and found that generally, when we use ChatGPT (latest models) we use 15ml per 100 generated words. But the ChatGPT or OpenAI in generally don't use it for literally drinking, but for cooling. 

Given the current environmental condition of our planet and our water consumption and waste, I decided that it will be an interesting project and mental experiment (guilt tripping basically) to create a visual way of knowing what's the non-monetary cost of our LLM usage for the most popular LLM platform in the world (currently). 

It is also my way of helping the environment through conscious use of this technology. Certainly for me, I try to use ChatGPT, and other platforms, such as Claude, less, exactly because of the guilt I feel. Idk, it works for me :)

And here is the extension. It is available at the google web store and Github. Free and open source.

### Key Studies & Reports:

-University of California, Riverside (2023): "Making AI Less Thirsty" - Study on water consumption in AI training and inference

-OpenAI Infrastructure Reports (2024): Analysis of GPT-4 operational costs including water usage

-Data Center Efficiency Studies (2024-2025): Industry reports on PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) and WUE (Water Usage Effectiveness)

-Google & Microsoft Sustainability Reports: Published water consumption metrics for cloud AI services
