# About

Recently I had to download many research studies from Arxiv and I found it really confusing and cumbersome to organize and rename all of my pdf formatted files. This is why I vibecoded arx-get.

It is completely free and available both on github and the google web store. Web store link [here](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/arx-get/jmefnenopbenhbdnkdgapihgnmfghejl?authuser=0&hl=en) and Github link [here](https://github.com/momchilgeorgiev/arx-get).
